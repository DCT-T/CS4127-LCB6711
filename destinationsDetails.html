Bruneian Culture

Learn some about Brunei culture that makes it unique.

Melayu Islam Beraja

Melayu Islam Beraja (MIB) is the national philosophy that blends the Malay and customs with the Islam with a sultanate that has laster over 600 years.

Respectful Culture

Respecting the elders, dressing modestly and warm hospitality are central in Brunei.

Local Cuisine

Experience local tastes!

Ambuyat

Ambuyat is a dish that is derived from the interior trunk of the sago palm. It is a starchy and bland substance similar to tapioca. It is often eaten with a two pronged bamboo utensile called a 'chandas' and served along witg flavourful side dishes and dipping sauces

Nasi Katok

Nasi Katok is a traditional fast food from Brunei Darussalam. It is made from rice, fried chicken and sambal. It is also served in a food wrap to eat on the go

Kueh Bahulu

Kueh Bahulu is a type of sponge cake that is often served during celebrations like Hari Raya and Weddings.

Travel Tips

These are some tips that are recommended when travelling to Brunei Darussalam.

Helpful Tips!

  • Alcohol is banned.
  • Dress Modestly.
  • Shops closes at 12pm-2pm for Friday prayers.
  • Public displays of affection are usually frowned on.
